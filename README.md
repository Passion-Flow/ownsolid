# ownsolid

**Your AI. Your foundation.** ownsolid is a self-hosted AI infrastructure platform
that runs entirely inside your network, including fully air-gapped environments.

One license, one login and one audit trail cover everything from the GPUs you own to
the models, agents and applications your teams use.

## What it brings together

- **Infrastructure:** nodes, accelerators and Kubernetes clusters under one control plane.
- **Models:** import, deploy, fine-tune and evaluate models offline, and serve them
  through an OpenAI-compatible gateway with keys, quotas and usage.
- **Applications:** workflows and agents, plugins, and a catalog of applications that
  install with one click and use the platform's models, identity and monitoring.
- **Governance:** enterprise sign-in, workspaces, role-based access and audit.
