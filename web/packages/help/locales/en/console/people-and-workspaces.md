# People and workspaces

People sign in to the console or the portal with their own accounts. Workspaces group people and
the work they share.

## Inviting people

On [Users](/users), choose **Invite**, enter the address and the language of the email. The
invitation stays open until it is accepted or expires; you can send it again or revoke it by typing
the address. Accounts are not deleted: disable an account instead.

## Disabling an account

Disabling asks for a reason and ends every session of that account at once. It can be undone, and
the person can sign in again after the account is enabled.

## Workspaces

[Workspaces](/users/workspaces) lists every workspace. Create one with an owner, rename it, disable
it or enable it again. Inside a workspace you add people by address, change their role or remove
them.

- Every workspace keeps at least one owner, so the last owner cannot be removed.
- The installation's default workspace cannot be disabled.
- Each change asks for a reason, which the [audit trail](help:audit-trail) records.
