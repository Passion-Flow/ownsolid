# Pin RKE2 component and notice evidence

Bind the reviewed RKE2 component SPDX document and its original licence
and publisher evidence to the same signed release that carries the
approved image sets. The main SPDX references the RKE2 document by its
namespace and checksum. The factory checks both pinned companion assets
and the document relationship during manufacture and verification.

New RKE2 manufacture requires the companions. Earlier signed packages
retain the compatible reader policy. Sites that need the component
inventory select the strict policy before preparing a package. Preserve
observations and original evidence separately from a production claim of
complete licence and corresponding-source coverage.

See the [component inventory research](../research/rke2-component-inventory.md).
